# Abe's Judging Criteria (v 2026-07-31)
Judge the LAST USER WHO COMMUNICATED (latest outbound call/SMS/email) — not the Lead Owner field.

## 1. Correct Status — bucket vs the MERCHANT'S words (read call/SMS/email content)
- "busy now" / "call later" / 1-3 days → 3 Day
- "few weeks" (1-3 wks) → 14 Day
- month+ / 4-6 weeks / any season or quarter / "interested but not now" / ~30+ days out → 40 Day
- live active deal being worked → High Priority
- Honored merchant timeline = Yes. Dead lead correctly parked = Yes. Days passing alone never = No.
- Agreed future reconnect (e.g. "fall") validates 40 Day even with unresolved product-fit.

## 2. Convo 2min> (MECHANICAL) — judged user had a call ≥120s in the 90d window.

## 3. Rep Error — BLANK is the common case; assign only when the error IS the story.
No connect to owner (never reached DM live; overrides all) · Status-too long (live thing
parked slow for weeks) · Sales Rep Conversation Skill (real convo botched: ignored stated
channel pref, broke agreed timeline, never asked app/time on live ask) · Didnt collect
information and was possible. When torn → blank.
PENDING ABE: "Missed Follow up" (used 3x, not yet on menu).

## 4. Task Verdict — task warranted only when merchant gave an exact time/date; check
open tasks under the judged user. time+task→Yes-Necessary · time+none→No-Should Have ·
no-time+task→Yes-Unnecessary · no-time+none→No-Not Needed.

## 5. Meaningful Touch — 2min+ call (or equivalent substantive exchange) covering what
the company does and/or revenue AND what Getty can offer. Transcript evidence counts.
Dials, voicemails, one-word replies, STOP = No.

## 6. Next Steps — ONE pick from convo+email content:
Follow up · Get App · Get Banks · Remove from positive · Adjust follow up date or status ·
Sales rep conversation training (merchant interested but rep didn't ask questions / no
follow-up to continue the conversation).

## 7. Rep Reported Revenue Filled (MECHANICAL) — custom field not empty and not "0. N/A".

## Calibration lanes (from Abe's corrections)
- Merchant-set slow/quarterly timeline → status Yes AND Remove from positive (off cadence).
- Ghosted promise: fresh (≤~6wks) → Follow up/Get Banks; stale (2+ mo, dodging, dead channels) → Remove.
- Materials/app already sent on a live deal → Follow up, not Get App.
- Unexplained reversal after full buy-in → may stay High Priority; next = Get App.
- Renewal-eligible past client in a cold cadence → status No (renewal track).
- Default status Yes for any defensible bucket; overturn only on clear contradiction.
